# CASEZERO — Enter the Unknown 🕵️

> Every truth begins with a question.

**CaseZero** is a cinematic mystery detective browser game where players investigate a crime scene, examine evidence, interrogate suspects, track investigation progress, and make a final accusation.

## 🎯 About the Project

CaseZero is a browser-based detective game built using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

Players investigate the disappearance of a journalist by examining physical clues, reviewing suspect statements, recording observations, and deciding who is responsible.

The game combines realistic investigation elements with a dark, cinematic mystery atmosphere.

**Project name:** CaseZero  
**Tagline:** Every truth begins with a question.  
**Project type:** Interactive browser game  
**Platform:** Web browser  
**Status:** Working prototype

## ✨ Features

- 🎬 Cinematic dark-themed interface with crimson accents.
- 🔍 Interactive crime-scene investigation.
- 🧩 Three pieces of evidence to examine.
- 👥 Three suspects with individual interview statements.
- 📊 Investigation progress tracker.
- 📁 Evidence board for reviewing clues.
- 📝 Detective notebook with automatic note saving.
- 🎯 Interactive final suspect selection.
- ⚖️ Different outcomes for correct and incorrect accusations.
- 📋 Investigation summary showing the accusation and number of suspects interrogated.
- 🔄 Play Again option to restart the game.
- 📱 Responsive layout for desktop and mobile-sized screens.

## 🕵️ Story and Case Details

A journalist has mysteriously disappeared. Before the disappearance, the journalist was involved in a financial dispute and was preparing to publish a sensitive document.

Three people may have information about what happened:

- A business partner involved in a financial argument.
- A neighbor who heard shouting late at night.
- An assistant who knew about the journalist's planned publication.

The detective must examine the evidence, compare timelines, identify inconsistencies, and submit a final accusation.

**Your mission:** Investigate the clues. Question the suspects. Uncover the truth.

*Note: This is a fictional mystery scenario. The current version is a prototype, and some forensic details remain intentionally unresolved.*

## 👤 Suspects

### 1. Adrian Cole — Business Partner

- Claims he left at **10:30 PM**.
- Admits arguing with the journalist about company finances.
- Denies returning after leaving.
- His timeline conflicts with the neighbor's report of an argument around 11:20 PM.

**Investigation question:** Is Adrian hiding something about his timeline?

### 2. Mira Hayes — Neighbor

- Reports hearing shouting around **11:20 PM**.
- Says she checked the time.
- Did not see anyone leave.

**Investigation question:** What exactly did Mira hear, and can her timeline be trusted?

### 3. Daniel Cross — Assistant

- Says the journalist was worried about a sensitive document.
- Suggests someone may have wanted to prevent its publication.
- Claims he left the office before **9:00 PM**.

**Investigation question:** Why does Daniel know about the document, and who else knew about it?

## 🧾 Evidence

| Evidence ID | Item | Description |
|---|---|---|
| E-001 | Broken wristwatch | A broken watch stopped at 11:47 PM. |
| E-002 | Half-filled coffee | A cup of coffee was left at the scene. |
| E-003 | Muddy footprint | A muddy footprint was found near the balcony. |

### Evidence interpretation

- **Broken wristwatch:** May be relevant to the timeline, but the reason it stopped is unknown.
- **Half-filled coffee:** May indicate someone's presence, but no forensic analysis is implemented.
- **Muddy footprint:** May suggest movement near the balcony, but its owner is unknown.

The player should compare these clues with the suspect statements before making an accusation.

## 🎮 How to Play

1. Open CaseZero in your browser.
2. Click **START INVESTIGATION**.
3. Click **EXAMINE CRIME SCENE** to reveal the clues.
4. Interview Adrian Cole, Mira Hayes, and Daniel Cross.
5. Review the evidence board.
6. Record your observations in the detective notebook.
7. Click **SOLVE THE CASE**.
8. Select the suspect you believe is responsible.
9. Click **CONFIRM ACCUSATION**.
10. Review the outcome and investigation
